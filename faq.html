Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about E+Learn. If you don't find what you're looking for, feel free to contact our support team.

General Questions

E+Learn is a comprehensive online learning platform designed to democratize education. We connect eager learners with expert instructors across a wide range of subjects, from technology and business to arts and languages, providing high-quality courses accessible from anywhere in the world.
Getting started is easy! Simply click the "Sign up" button in the top right corner to create a free account. Once registered, you can browse our course catalog, enroll in free or paid courses, and start learning immediately from your dashboard.
Creating an account on E+Learn is completely free. We offer a wide variety of free courses that you can take at no cost. Premium courses require a one-time purchase or a subscription, but we always ensure pricing is transparent before you enroll.
E+Learn is fully responsive and works on any device with a modern web browser. Whether you prefer learning on a desktop, laptop, tablet, or smartphone, your progress syncs automatically across all your devices so you can learn on the go.

Courses & Learning

For Teachers

We are always looking for experts! Visit our "Become an Instructor" page to apply. You'll need to submit a brief bio and a sample lesson. Our team reviews applications within 3-5 business days.
Our instructor dashboard provides a robust course builder where you can upload videos, create interactive quizzes, upload PDFs, and manage student discussions. We also offer a resource library with tips on recording high-quality audio and video.
Instructors earn a revenue share on every paid enrollment. The exact percentage depends on whether the student found the course through our organic marketing or your own referral link. Top instructors on E+Learn earn full-time incomes.

Billing & Payments

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and in select regions, Apple Pay and Google Pay. All transactions are secured with SSL encryption.
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all courses. If you are not satisfied with your purchase for any reason, you can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase, provided you haven't completed more than 30% of the course.
No hidden fees. The price you see on the course page is the price you pay. We do not charge enrollment fees, cancellation fees, or maintenance fees.
If you are subscribed to our "E+Learn Pro" membership, you can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. You will retain access to Pro features until the end of your current billing cycle.
